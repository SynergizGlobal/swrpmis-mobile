Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Applies to the SWR-PMIS mobile app on Android and iOS.

This Privacy Policy explains how SWR-PMIS by Synergiz (shown on your device as SWR-PMIS) handles information. The app is published by SYNERGIZ GLOBAL SERVICES PRIVATE LIMITED (“we”, “us”).

About the app

SWR-PMIS is a Project Management Information System for authorized users only. It helps users view and update project information such as works, RFI, issues, forms, reports, and related records. Information entered in the app is sent to the PMIS servers used by the service.

Information we collect

  • Account details — login ID, password (used only to sign in), and profile information returned by the PMIS system after login (such as name or role, when available).
  • Work data — project, RFI, issue, form, and report data you view or submit while using the app.
  • Device / push token — if you allow notifications, a push token and basic device info (for example platform and an app-generated device id) so we can send work-related alerts.
  • App data on your device — short-term login session information and basic preferences (for example theme) stored on your device to keep you signed in and remember your choices.

We do not sell your personal data.

What we do not collect

At this time, SWR-PMIS does not collect:

  • precise location
  • contacts, SMS, or call logs
  • microphone audio
  • payment or card details
  • advertising identifiers for ads

If a future feature needs camera, photos, files, or location, we will ask for permission at that time and update this policy before enabling it.

Permissions

The app may ask for:

  • Internet — to sign in and sync with PMIS servers.
  • Notifications — to send optional work-related alerts. You can deny or turn this off in system settings.

The app does not currently request camera, microphone, contacts, or location permission.

Push notifications and Firebase

If notifications are enabled, we use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver alerts. We may also use Firebase Remote Config for basic app settings such as update messages, and Firebase Crashlytics for crash and stability reports (for example device type, app version, and error details) so we can fix faults. These services are used only to run and maintain the app, not for advertising.

Ads and tracking

SWR-PMIS has no advertisements and does not track you across other apps or websites for advertising.

How we use information

  • to sign you in and keep your session active
  • to show and update PMIS information you are allowed to use
  • to send optional work-related notifications you allow
  • to keep the app working securely
  • when required by law

Project records are stored on the PMIS servers. We do not share your personal data with third parties for their marketing.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, including secure network connections where supported. How long data is kept follows the rules of the PMIS service and applicable law. Session data on your device is removed when you sign out or clear app data. Push tokens are deactivated when you sign out.

Your choices

You can sign out of the app and manage notification permission in your device settings. For access, correction, or deletion of personal data held in the PMIS system, contact us by email. We may need to verify your identity and may work with your organization to complete the request.

Children

SWR-PMIS is for authorized adult professional users and is not intended for children under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date above and publish the updated page at the same public web address used in the app stores.
