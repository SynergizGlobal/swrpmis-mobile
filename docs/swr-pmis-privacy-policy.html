Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This policy describes how SWR-PMIS by Synergiz (shown on your device as SWR-PMIS) handles information. The app is published by SYNERGIZ GLOBAL SERVICES PRIVATE LIMITED (“we”, “us”).

About the app

SWR-PMIS is for authorized users only. It supports project and construction management (PMIS) for South Western Railway—such as projects, works, issues, forms, and related records—and Request for Inspection (RFI) workflows. Data you enter is sent to official PMIS servers over a secure connection.

Information we collect

  • Account details — login ID, name, role, and related profile fields.
  • Work data — forms, remarks, and status updates you submit in PMIS or RFI.
  • Location — only when you use a feature that needs it (for example, site location for an RFI inspection), and only while you are using the app.
  • Photos and files — images or documents you choose to capture or attach.
  • Push token — if you allow notifications, a token so we can send work-related alerts (see below).
  • Crash reports — basic device and error information to fix app problems (see below).

We do not sell your data.

Device permissions

The app asks for access only when needed:

  • Internet — sign in and sync with PMIS / RFI servers.
  • Notifications — optional work-related alerts. You can deny or turn this off in system settings.
  • Camera — site photos and inspection images.
  • Location (while using the app) — inspection site location when required.
  • Photos / files — attachments you select; we do not browse all files on your device.

We do not use the microphone, contacts, SMS, background location, or phone identity for this app.

Tracking and advertising

The app has no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not use your data for advertising.

Crash reporting

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive anonymous crash and stability reports (for example, device type, app version, and error details). This helps us fix faults. We do not use it for ads or marketing analytics.

Push notifications

If you enable notifications, we use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver work-related alerts. We may use Firebase Remote Config for basic app settings such as update messages. These services are used only to run and maintain the app, not for advertising. Push tokens are deactivated when you sign out.

How we use and share data

  • To run PMIS and RFI features you are allowed to use.
  • To send optional work-related notifications you allow.
  • With authorized project systems and users as part of official work.
  • When required by law.

Business records are stored on project servers. The app may keep short-term session or draft data on your device.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections where supported. Retention follows project and legal rules. Crash logs are kept only as long as needed to maintain the app.

Your choices

You can deny camera, location, or notification permission; some features may not work. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by emailing us. We may verify your identity and work with your organization where needed.

Children

The app is for authorized professional users and is not aimed at children under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date above and publish the updated page at the same public web address.
